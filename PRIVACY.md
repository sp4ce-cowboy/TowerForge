# Tiny Frontline: privacy policy

*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

Tiny Frontline is the single-player App Store release of TowerForge, a game made by a team of
four at the National University of Singapore, published by
[sp4ce-cowboy](https://github.com/sp4ce-cowboy).

**Tiny Frontline doesn't collect any data.**

- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- The game doesn't connect to the internet.
- Your progress (statistics, achievements, missions and rank) is saved only on your iPad.
  Deleting the app deletes it.
- The app contains no third-party code or services.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first.

Questions: open an issue at
[github.com/sp4ce-cowboy/TowerForge/issues](https://github.com/sp4ce-cowboy/TowerForge/issues).
