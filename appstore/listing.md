## Description [4000]

Tiny Frontline is a lane-based strategy game for iPad. Spend coins to send pixel troops down
the lanes, build arrow towers to hold your line, and break through before the enemy breaks
through yours.

FEATURES
• Three modes: Death Match, Capture the Flag and Survival.
• Melee fighters, soldiers, wizards and arrow towers, each with its own speed, attack and health.
• Power-ups: free units, a damage boost and invulnerability.
• An AI opponent that sends its own troops down random lanes at random times.
• Ranks from Private to General, earned with XP, plus achievements and missions.
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. Works offline.

Tiny Frontline started as TowerForge, a team project by four students at the National
University of Singapore: Keith Gan, Phua Zheng Ze, Rubesh Suresh and Vanessa Mae. This is its
single-player edition.

## App Review notes

No account or sign-in is needed. Tap Start, then choose Death Match, Capture the Flag or
Survival (Survival asks for a level). In a game, tap a unit card at the bottom, then tap a
lane to send that unit. The Player screen shows rank, achievements and missions. The original
project's online multiplayer and leaderboards are not part of this version.
